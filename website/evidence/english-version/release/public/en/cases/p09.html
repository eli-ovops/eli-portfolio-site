Furniture barcode inventory system

Led a carton-based barcode inventory system, designing receiving, transfers and dispatch with permissions, duplicate-submission handling and correction flows.

Overview

ContextMy roleWork deliveredApproach

Context

Organized inventory operations by carton, making quantities verifiable across receiving, transfers, dispatch and corrections.

My role

Led requirements analysis and workflow design, used AI-assisted development, and handled testing and ongoing maintenance.

Work delivered

Carton scanning, receiving, dispatch, transfers, permissions, duplicate-submission handling and reversal of mistakes.

Approach

In a simulated inventory demo, 10 cartons were received, 4 transferred and 3 dispatched, leaving 7 in total. Specific quantities were used to verify each operation.
