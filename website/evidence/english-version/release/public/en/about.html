FORWARD DEPLOYED ENGINEERING

Understand the business.
Build the solution.

For me, forward deployed engineering means working closely with a business, contributing to implementation and following how the solution performs in practice.

Real requirements often emerge from everyday work. Through observation, interviews and collaboration, I identify repetitive tasks, gaps in information and practical constraints, then turn them into testable workflows and software.

Business understanding
Understand responsibilities, business terminology and data sources; define the problem, priorities and scope.
Engineering
Turn workflows into prototypes and working systems, with attention to data, AI integration and the operating environment.
Iterative delivery
Refine requirements through demonstrations and user feedback, validate in stages and retain test and acceptance records.
Ongoing collaboration
Plan training, handover and maintenance so the solution can keep working and evolve.

From discovery to review

  1. Discovery

    Observe workflows and understand repetitive tasks and practical difficulties.

  2. Use-case selection

    Choose the first use case based on business value, available data and implementation cost.

  3. Prototype validation

    Use a demonstrable prototype to clarify the workflow and scope, and gather feedback early.

  4. Iteration

    Adjust features based on real use and progress through testing and acceptance.

  5. Handover

    Organize code, documentation and operating instructions for handover and version management.

  6. Review

    Review results and unresolved issues to guide the next iteration.
