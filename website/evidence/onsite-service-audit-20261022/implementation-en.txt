  - heading "What does an FDE do?" [level=2]
  - paragraph: FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer. The work starts with the customer’s operating context, connecting requirements, tool selection, implementation and feedback.
  - paragraph: We can map who performs a task, what goes in and who uses the result. Start with an existing tool when it fits; discuss custom data connections or interfaces when needed.
  - heading "What can we discuss first?" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Repeated spreadsheet handling, inventory checks, scattered product information, recurring support questions and product content preparation are possible starting points. Choose a task with clear inputs, outputs and an owner.
  - paragraph: Describe the current process, its most time-consuming step and the result you want. Redacted examples can help. You do not need to buy a tool or share passwords or a complete business dataset first.
  - heading "From discussion to a bounded pilot" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Map repeated handling and common mistakes, then compare existing tools, rule-based automation and AI assistance. Whether a tool such as WorkBuddy fits depends on the task, data requirements and cost.
  - paragraph: Agree the pilot’s features, human review steps, handover materials and acceptance criteria before implementation. Narrow the scope or change the approach if the trial does not fit.
  - heading "How do we assess the result?" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Record comparable task duration, repeated entry or reasons for rework before implementation. Use the same measures during the pilot and check whether staff can continue using it and handle exceptions.
  - paragraph: The initial conversation is free. Implementation services start at CNY 500, with scope and fees agreed before work begins. Agree tool subscriptions, development, training and maintenance separately, then assess value against observed pilot results.
