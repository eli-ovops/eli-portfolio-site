  - heading "Which step should change first?" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Look for frequent work with reasonably clear rules and checkable inputs and outputs. Record who owns it, how long it takes and why rework happens. Identify repeated transfer or checking.
  - paragraph: Include subscriptions, staff learning, integrations and maintenance in the cost. A small change that works can be enough; do not add process just to use AI.
  - heading "Entry and inventory checks" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Discuss duplicate entry, scattered stock movement records or manual reconciliation. Confirm business rules and access before choosing existing tools or custom inventory software.
  - paragraph: Quantities, documents and movement records must reconcile. Define correction, reversal and activity records. Use software validation for fixed rules and assess AI when unstructured content needs interpretation.
  - heading "Product information, copy and images" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Organize product facts, specifications and reusable assets, then assess AI for drafting copy or preparing images. A responsible person checks prices, specifications and brand wording before publication.
  - paragraph: Try one product type or channel. Check source completeness and the amount of editing required. Generation, human review and publication are separate steps with separate checks.
  - heading "Check the improvement, not just the tool" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Compare similar tasks before and after the change for duration, repeated entry and rework while checking correctness. Staff understanding and recovery from mistakes are part of acceptance.
  - paragraph: Agree pilot scope and cost first, then expand only if results justify it. Existing tools such as WorkBuddy can be a starting point. Agree custom development, training and maintenance separately.

  - heading "Does efficiency require AI?" [level=3]
  - paragraph: No. Forms, validation or conventional software may fit fixed rules better. Choose the approach for the business task first.
  - heading "Can you promise a specific saving?" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Assess your actual tasks in a pilot. Agree comparison measures, then record correctness, duration and maintenance cost before claiming improvement.

  - paragraph: Describe the current process, its slowest step and the result you want.
